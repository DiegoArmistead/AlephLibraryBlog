A letter

Why Aleph

In the beginning there was nothing. Then, an idea. And now Aleph is about to cross from the field of ideas into the world.

Reading and writing are a pillar of human society: they're how knowledge survives from one generation to the next. So isn't it strange how little the everyday experience of it has changed? For most of us, we still read the way we did in 2010: highlight, jot a note, and watch most of those notes drift into oblivion. A few tools have tried to change that, but they sit apart from your everyday reading, or turn it into work.

There's always a reason we do what we do. We read out of curiosity: to understand more, to answer our questions, to create and share, to become better. And yet, even in the digital era, this side of reading hasn't been given the focus it deserves. We have all the knowledge we could want within reach, but how much of it actually stays with us? How easy is it, really, to own, retain, connect, and assimilate what we read?

It's not about which books you read. It's about which idea you want to understand.

Aleph is built with one core intention: to give you the tools to truly assimilate what you read into your own thinking. To make the knowledge yours, and to use it, whether to create something and add your grain of sand to the vast ocean of human knowledge, or simply to share it.

Because there's a conversation out there: a great one, between authors across centuries. And you can pull up a chair and take part. They aren't comparing which books they've read; they're wrestling with ideas. That's why you read, too: to appreciate, to understand, and to give shape to ideas of your own.

On November 1, Aleph sees its first sunrise on the App Store. It's built to work offline, Apple-first and privacy-first: your data stays on your device, never mined by corporations to build a profile and sell you things. It's made to harvest, connect, and assimilate what you read, so your highlights and notes are never buried in oblivion, but within reach every time you connect a new idea. Aleph brings purpose to reading, helping you make the most of every session and truly own the knowledge in your personal library.

We'll be listening closely and answering as fast as we can, hoping to give you a way of reading worthy of this era: one that turns the device in your hand into something that helps you grow and reach what you set out to learn.

Read, assimilate, create.
— Diego, and the team at Aleph
