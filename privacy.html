Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Aleph is built privacy-first. Your reading—your library, highlights, notes, and the connections you make—lives on your device and in your own iCloud. We do not run a server that collects it, we do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we do not track you.

This policy explains what Aleph ("the app") does with your information. Aleph is developed by Diego Zárate Rosales ("we", "us", "our"). It applies to the Aleph iOS app and to this website, alephlibrary.com.

Our approach

Aleph is designed to keep your data with you. The app works on-device and syncs only through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit private database). We, the developer, have no access to that data. There is no Aleph analytics server, no third-party advertising SDK, and no cross-app tracking.

What the app handles, and where it lives

  • Your reading data — the books/PDFs you add, highlights, notes, hand-drawn annotations, tags, reading sessions, and the connections in your graph. Stored on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your private iCloud (CloudKit). Your document files live in your own iCloud Drive / Files. We never receive them.
  • Sign in with Apple — if you create an account, we use Apple's Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app a private identifier; you can choose to hide your email. You can delete your account and its data from within the app.
  • Mood / State of Mind (optional) — if you log how a reading session felt, and you grant permission, the app writes that to Apple HealthKit as a State of Mind entry, stored in Apple Health on your device. See the HealthKit section below.
  • Location (optional) — if you enable the reading map, the app may capture an approximate location (about 100 m, "while using the app", one-shot) when you finish a session, to place a pin. It is stored with your session in your own data. It is never continuously tracked and never shared.
  • Photos (optional) — if you attach a photo to a reading session, it is stored with your data. The app only accesses photos you explicitly pick.
  • Weather (optional) — the reading map may show the weather for a session using Apple WeatherKit. Weather data is provided by Apple.

On-device intelligence

Aleph's suggestions and summaries use Apple Intelligence's on-device models (the Foundation Models framework) and on-device embeddings. This processing happens on your device. Your library content is not sent to us or to any third party to power these features.

HealthKit

If you use mood logging, Aleph reads and writes only your State of Mind entries through HealthKit, with your permission. We handle this data according to Apple's requirements: we do not use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, we do not share it with third parties, and it is never sent off your device by us. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Apple services we rely on

Because Aleph is an Apple-only app, it uses Apple frameworks and services on your behalf: iCloud / CloudKit (sync of your private data), Sign in with Apple (accounts), HealthKit (mood), WeatherKit (weather), and Core Location (optional map pins). Your use of these is also governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Weather data shown in the app is © Apple Weather.

This website and the waitlist

If you enter your email in the "Notify me" form on this website, we use Buttondown to store it and to send you a single notification when Aleph launches, plus occasional updates you can unsubscribe from at any time. We use your email only for this. We do not sell it or share it beyond Buttondown, which acts as our email processor. This website itself does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app does not use the Advertising Identifier.
  • We do not use your reading, health, or location data to build advertising profiles.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data stays until you delete it. Deleting the app removes local data; data you synced to iCloud is managed in your Apple ID / iCloud settings. If you created an account, you can delete it and its associated data from within the app. To remove your email from the waitlist, use the unsubscribe link in any email or contact us.

Your rights by region

Depending on where you live, you may have specific rights over your personal data. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. If you are in California, you have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion, and the right to know that we do not sell your personal information—we don't. Because Aleph keeps your data on your device and in your own iCloud, most of it is never in our possession. To exercise any right, or simply to ask a question, email support@alephlibrary.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Aleph is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@alephlibrary.com.
